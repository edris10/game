About Block Game

Welcome to Block Game, a challenging puzzle game where you navigate through levels by moving blocks and reaching the goal!

Game Features

  • Multiple challenging levels with increasing difficulty
  • Simple controls - move left/right and drop blocks
  • Progress saving - your completed levels are saved
  • Responsive design - play on desktop or mobile devices
  • Engaging sound effects for better gameplay experience

How to Play

Use the left and right buttons to move your character. Press the down button to pick up or drop blocks. Your goal is to reach the golden circle in each level by strategically moving blocks to create a path.

About the Developer

This game was created as a fun project to challenge players' problem-solving skills. The developer enjoys creating interactive experiences that are both entertaining and mentally stimulating.
